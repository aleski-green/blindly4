summarizes the project's intended offering.

## Community use

Personal and non-commercial use, and use for your own internal business purposes,
are permitted within the license terms. Modifications are permitted for those uses.
Distribution must be free of charge for non-commercial purposes, include the
license and required notices, and prominently identify modifications.

Existing functionality remains community-licensed, including any existing
collaboration or automation-monitoring functionality. A feature name, roadmap
entry, or sales offering alone does not make existing code enterprise-only.

## Commercial offering

We plan to offer paid cloud hosting, maintenance, consulting, bug fixing,
collaborative features, and automation monitoring. Contact
[license@sapiens4.ai](mailto:license@sapiens4.ai) for commercial agreements.
The community license does not promise a hosted service, support, fixes, uptime,
or a service-level agreement.

Other providers may charge for consulting, maintenance, and bug-fixing services
where the underlying software use complies with the community license. These
services are not exclusive to us. A services contract does not independently
permit commercial redistribution or customer-facing hosted access.

Operating a paid hosted version, reselling or white-labeling the software, or
embedding customer-facing agent/computer-control capabilities may require a
separate commercial agreement. Contact us with the actual architecture and
customer access model before relying on the community license for such use.

## Future enterprise additions

Future enterprise-only collaboration or automation-monitoring additions must
receive a separate license with an identified licensor and explicit file or
directory coverage before release. This change creates no enterprise-only files
and does not change the license of existing community functionality.
A commercial service offering does not itself reserve source code.
